How Application Works:
---------------------> 
Fpi is about forign investores who need to create invest in capital market in india they need to create an application 
in Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
After the application get approval they can manage trading and can buy and sell in market
    
Category I FPIs include the lowest risk entities, such as foreign governments and government-related foreign investors. 
Category II FPIs include appropriately regulated broad-based funds, 
appropriately regulated entities, broad-based funds whose investment manager
 is appropriately regulated, university funds, pension funds, etc.
 Category III FPIs include others not eligible under the first two categories, 
 such as individuals, corporates, family offices, among others.
